SharePoint Online · PII Exposure Scanner

Search accessible SharePoint content for potential sensitive-information exposure and export a findings register.

Important: This is a discovery aid, not a DLP or compliance certification tool. Search results indicate possible matches and require human review. The app records location metadata only; it does not download, display, or export document contents or the matched text. Search is permission-trimmed to the signed-in account.
